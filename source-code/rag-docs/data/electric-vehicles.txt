Electric cars store power in large lithium batteries and drive with
quiet motors. A home charger adds about 40 miles of range per hour,
so most drivers fill up overnight. Charging from rooftop solar makes
each mile nearly carbon free. Public fast chargers add 200 miles in
about 20 minutes for road trips. Battery range grows each year as
cell density climbs and prices fall. Used battery packs get a second
life as home storage before recycling.
