Carbon dioxide from fossil fuel traps heat in the air. Global mean
temperature has risen about 1.2 degrees since 1900. Cutting coal, oil,
and gas use slows further warming. Methane leaks matter too, since
methane warms far more per ton than carbon dioxide in the short term.
Cities track per capita emissions to rank climate plans. Home solar,
heat pumps, and electric cars form the fastest household path to deep
cuts in carbon output.
